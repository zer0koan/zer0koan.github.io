Zerokoan Research is a personal archive of security research.

Far be it for me to explain what a Buddhist koan is, but Zerokoan came from an early understanding of what they signify: Abandonment of analytical reason to transcend logic, forcing awareness through intuition over intellect. This is more than likely both an incorrect and glib explanation. Zerokoan is the opposite: Knowing what and why, right now.

Ultimately, it's a doomed endeavor. I'm sure that's obvious, but I was nothing if not obstinate in my youth. I still am, but I was then, too. I'm not sure I can ever leave behind the need to know everything.
